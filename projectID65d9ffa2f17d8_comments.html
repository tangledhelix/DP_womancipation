About the Book

Translation of the French original, in which Adele Crepaz - 1849-1919 - an Austrian journalist, argues against women's suffrage because women's innate superiority would displace men (right?!)

Printed in 1893.

Formatting Instructions

In the letter at the start, please put right-aligned content in its own no-wraps, leaving out the greeting. Do not use no-wraps for the greeting. Treat the attribution at the end of it as a footnote even though there is no anchor.

Otherwise, follow the normal formatting guidelines.
